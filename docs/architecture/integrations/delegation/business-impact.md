# Delegation Business Impact

Delegation can improve throughput and reduce bottlenecks by moving work to the system or team best suited to handle it. It also introduces coordination costs that need to be managed carefully.

## Business Value

- Speeds up processing by routing tasks to the right owner.
- Reduces manual follow-up and email-based handoffs.
- Improves accountability through explicit ownership and audit trails.
- Helps scale workflows that would otherwise depend on a central team.

## Risks if Not Addressed

- Poor routing can delay work or send it to the wrong destination.
- Missing context can cause the delegated system to fail or make bad decisions.
- Unclear ownership can create gaps in accountability.
- Incomplete audit trails can make investigations difficult.

## Success Metrics

- Lower average time to complete delegated tasks.
- Fewer manual escalations and reassignments.
- Reduced number of delegation failures or timeouts.
- Clearer ownership captured in operational reporting.

## Stakeholders

- Operations teams that monitor workflow health.
- Product owners who define routing and approval rules.
- Support teams that handle exceptions and escalations.
