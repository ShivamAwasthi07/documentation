# Reference Data Business Impact

Reference data integration has a direct effect on consistency, operational effort, and reporting accuracy. When shared values drift between systems, teams spend time correcting mismatches instead of delivering features.

## Business Value

- Improves data consistency across channels and applications.
- Lowers manual maintenance for shared codes and lookup tables.
- Supports cleaner reporting and more reliable analytics.
- Reduces user-facing errors caused by mismatched values.

## Risks if Not Addressed

- Different systems may show conflicting values.
- Manual fixes can introduce delays and hidden defects.
- Reporting and reconciliation become harder to trust.
- Consumer systems may reject records because of invalid codes.

## Success Metrics

- Reduction in data correction tickets.
- Lower rate of validation failures tied to reference values.
- Faster turnaround for updating shared lookup sets.
- Fewer discrepancies found in reconciliation or reporting.

## Stakeholders

- Product teams that rely on validated shared values.
- Operations teams that manage updates and exceptions.
- Data and analytics teams that depend on consistent mappings.
