## Business Impact

- Reduces manual documentation effort and the time engineers and product teams spend writing and maintaining docs.
- Improves consistency and quality of documentation by using templates and automated formatting rules.
- Accelerates development readiness by ensuring requirements are transformed into developer-friendly artifacts as soon as stories are accepted.
- Increases traceability and auditability by linking Rally stories to GitHub pull requests and preserving story metadata in the repository.
- Enables teams to focus more on building features while maintaining up-to-date, discoverable documentation in code repositories, which can reduce onboarding time and lower the risk of misinterpretation of requirements.
